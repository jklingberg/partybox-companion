## Hypotheses under test

Stated before the run so the result can falsify them.

### Mechanism A — "not in Spotify" is `audio_ready` with extra steps

librespot is the only process advertising Spotify Connect over Zeroconf, and
`_gate_spotify_on_audio` (`packages/companion/src/companion/__main__.py`) runs
it *only* while `audio_ready` is true plus `_AUDIO_GRACE_SECONDS` (300 s).

> **A1.** Any A2DP outage longer than 5 minutes removes PartyBox from every
> Spotify client's device list.
> **A2.** Recovery requires the full chain A2DP → librespot cold start →
> Zeroconf re-announce → client rediscovery, and the last link is client-side
> and outside our control.
> **A3.** After an *unclean* Pi power cut specifically, A2DP reconnection is
> additionally delayed by the speaker still holding a stale link to the
> vanished host, and librespot's Spotify login may fail against a
> pre-NTP `fake-hwclock` date.

### Mechanism B — "stutter" is the BLE reconnect loop stealing the radio

ADR-028 records a 2026-07-21 `btmon` capture proving the LE scan *start/stop
mode switch* on this BCM4345 combo controller costs a ~440 ms cluster of A2DP
TX gaps, independent of scan duration. `AudioFocusService` was gated to skip
scanning outright while streaming as a result. `DeviceManager`'s reconnect
loop was not: `_connect_and_maintain` calls `_scan()` unconditionally each
pass with a 5 s → 60 s backoff, and its `streaming_fn` only stretches the
*health-check* interval.

> **B1.** Whenever the BLE control link is down while A2DP audio plays, the
> appliance produces an audible click every 5 s ramping to every 60 s, for as
> long as the link stays down.
> **B2.** Anything that takes the speaker's single BLE central slot (the JBL
> app, another central) puts the appliance in exactly that state, which is why
> "other Bluetooth devices" correlates with stutter.
> **B3.** During an ADR-042 link-establishment storm (observed up to ~2 h 53 m)
> this would be continuous for the whole window.

**PHYS-08 is the decisive experiment for B**: the JBL app takes BLE while A2DP
stays up, so if the scan loop is innocent, audio is unaffected.

### Mechanism B′ — the healthy state may click once a minute

`_STREAMING_HEALTH_CHECK_INTERVAL` in `manager.py` exists because the *same*
2026-07-21 btmon session independently caught the health probe's GATT
round-trip as an audible click source, landing "exactly every ~15.2 s […]
with zero relation to any AudioFocusService activity". It was relaxed 15 s →
60 s while streaming. But the parallel FDDF finding on this controller was
that cadence cuts click *frequency* and never per-click *severity* — the
reason FDDF scanning was eventually gated off outright rather than slowed.

> **B′.** A fully healthy, fully connected, otherwise-idle appliance still
> emits an audible click roughly every 60 s while streaming, indefinitely.

If B′ holds, "the music stutters" may be partly a **steady-state** defect
present with no other Bluetooth device in the room, and every other stutter
observation this session must be read as sitting on top of that baseline.
PHYS-12 tests it first, before anything is perturbed.
